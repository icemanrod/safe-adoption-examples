# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree about the work, not about the person
doing it.

## Specifically

- Technical criticism is welcome and wanted. "This finding does not follow from
  the evidence" is a contribution.
- Assume the other person read carefully and reached a different conclusion,
  until you have reason to think otherwise.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments are not welcome and will
  result in removal.

## Reporting

Email **hello@icemanrod.com**. Reports are read by one person and handled
privately.
